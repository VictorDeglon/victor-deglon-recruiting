  <h2>Bio</h2>
  <p>
    I started playing volleyball in June 2024 — rec ball at the beach
    with family and friends, then a few camps at Seaside/San Diego
    Volleyball Club that turned into a spot on their boys U14 local
    team, playing 3s. A few days before a tournament I got bumped up
    to 2s and played there through the end of fall season; that
    spring I moved up to the 1s, the top locals team, before dropping
    back down due to an injury.
  </p>
  <p>
    That's when I started a real weights-and-jump training program
    and began taking privates at Coast with Owen Adams, plus beach
    and indoor camps at Coast. I tried out for Seaside's travel
    program and got offered a spot on Seaside 14 Black — the best
    14U team at Seaside, and the only Seaside team playing the Open
    division. We placed 20th at Nationals after losing to Aspire 14
    Groot in the playoffs.
  </p>
  <p>
    After Nationals I was playing up to 26 hours a week across lifts,
    Coast, Seaside, and beach volleyball, and spent that summer
    rebuilding my jump serve — I had a fast arm but no control over
    it yet. I tried out for Coast and was offered a spot on the
    15-1s, which I took. This year I'll be wearing #8 again.
  </p>

  <h2>Work ethic</h2>
  <p>
    My goal at every practice is to get better as fast as I can —
    I've kept training through quad and tricep tendonitis and shin
    splints to do it. Every touch is either to score or to get
    better trying. I watch film and talk to people who know more
    than me about what to improve, and I talk to my coaches outside
    of practice to figure out what to work on before the next one.
  </p>
