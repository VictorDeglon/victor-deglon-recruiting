  <h1>Training</h1>
  <p class="lede">
    I named it "Greek Fire" when I was younger — the name stuck even
    though I'll admit it's a little dramatic. The goal is a
    well-rounded, athletic, thick-muscle-mass hybrid build: not built
    for straight-line speed, but built to be extremely explosive.
  </p>

  <h2>What it's built around</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Plyometrics</strong> — jump training on leg days, feeding directly into the vertical/approach numbers on the <a href="{{ '/stats/' | relative_url }}">stats page</a>.</li>
    <li><strong>Heavy lifts</strong> — the base strength work the explosiveness is built on.</li>
    <li><strong>HIIT</strong> — conditioning work layered in around practice schedule.</li>
    <li><strong>Swim</strong> — low-impact conditioning, folded into pull days.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>
    Current build: 187 lbs (~84.8 kg) at roughly 14% body fat.
  </p>

  <h2>Weekly structure</h2>
  <p>
    A pretty generic Push / Pull / Legs split, adjusted week to week
    around practice schedule — Legs includes the plyometric work,
    Pull includes swim.
  </p>
